Statistics modal — where the three new cards go

Same three Tier-A cards in every variant — peak focus hours (heatmap), focus rhythm (session-length histogram + break discipline) and consistency & momentum (composite score + deltas + 8-week trend). Only their placement changes. Everything else in the panel (the four KPI tiles, Daily Activity, By Subject, Recent Sessions, the footer) is the shipped markup and the shipped CSS, copied verbatim — so the only thing you're judging is structure. Click a variant below.

Data: the browser profile I checked has no timerStats yet, so this page computes from a deterministic seeded dataset of ~1,900 sessions over 90 days in the real session shape { type, duration, subject, date } — same fields logSession() writes. Hour of day is derived honestly, the way it would ship: new Date(s.date) − s.duration (the stored timestamp is stamped at completion). Momentum = 100 × (0.45·volume + 0.30·active-days + 0.25·avg-length), each term capped, vs the previous 7 days. Nothing here reads or writes your real stats. One design detail shown in all four variants, flagged for your call: the heatmap is drawn over the last 8 weeks rather than following the Today / This Week / All Time pills — hourly patterns need weeks of data, and a single-day heatmap is nearly empty.
