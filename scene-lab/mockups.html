UI direction mockups

Four candidate directions for the Pomora overhaul, each showing every surface (small settings with all three clock options, release notes, confirm popup, statistics, planner at all three depths, add-task, editor chrome). Motion (springy/subtle) and font (Plus Jakarta / Inter) are live toggles on every page. Pick a direction + one option per variant group. Throwaway files — nothing here ships.

RefinedEvolution of today's look. Same dark-glass identity, tightened: consistent 9/12/18 radii, unified hairline borders, tidy spacing, the site's indigo accent. The safest, most Pomora direction. Lighter & FlatterA genuinely light theme: solid white panels, crisp hairlines, almost no blur or glow. Calm, paper-like, maximal readability. Try the font toggle here — Inter was made for this. Bolder & ExpressiveLeans into the animated-scene identity: violet-lit surfaces, glowing accents, bigger radii, more atmosphere. The most personality — and the most visual noise behind a study timer. Raycast-softThe reference feel: soft charcoal panels with quiet depth, generous radii, a warm coral accent, springy micro-interactions. App-like, cozy, premium — your stated reference.
