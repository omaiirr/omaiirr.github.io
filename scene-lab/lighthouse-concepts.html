Lighthouse rework — concept stills

Three static composition variations for the realistic-seascape direction. Same painter stack as the final scene (layered gradients, additive glow, blurred mist, cached tower render). Default palette shown: beam #ffe1a8 on #0a0e1a. Pick A, B, or C — the winner becomes the baseline the animated scene is built from.

A — Moonlit calm

Moon upper-left with layered corona; glassy far sea with a long moon glint column; thin mist band; beam sweeping mid-seaward. The quietest, most hypnotic read.

B — Cloud banks

Heavier drifting cloud layers with moonlit rims; the beam catches one cloud's underside with a warm patch; moderate mist; beam framed against open sky right of the tower. Most atmospheric depth.

C — Storm hero

Tighter hero crop: bigger tower, rougher foreground sea with foam and rock surf, thicker low mist, darker sky. The beam cuts through haze above the swell. Most dramatic.
